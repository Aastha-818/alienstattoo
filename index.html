01how aliens started

“you don’t havethat thingin you.”

that’s what an artist told our founder, sunny bhanushali, the first time a friend walked him into a tattoo studio. he was a graphic designer from dombivli who drew in charcoal at night. he kept the sentence, and in 2011 he opened aliens in mumbai. every piece on our floor since has been the reply.
